Moving Walk

Smooth People Flow in Busy Spaces

An escalator is a vertical transportation in the form of a conveyor to transport people, which consists of separate stairs that can move up and down following a track in the form of a rail or chain driven by a motor.

Because it is driven by an electric motor, this escalator is designed to carry people from the bottom up or vice versa. For short distances escalators are used all over the world to transport pedestrians where using elevators is impractical. Its use is mainly in shopping centers, airports, transit systems, convention centers, hotels and other public facilities.
FUJI SANEI Escalator series are manufactured with the new material and the leading technology and the advanced design according to the china national standard GB16899 -1997 (the manufacture of escalator and moving walk and the installation safety criterion) and Europe standard EN115.
